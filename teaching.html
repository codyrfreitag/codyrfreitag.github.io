Teaching

I enjoy helping students develop an understanding of the mathematical ideas and ways of thinking that underpin computer science. One of the most rewarding aspects of teaching is seeing students gain the confidence to approach unfamiliar problems and make connections between ideas.

I'm particularly interested in teaching core theory courses such as discrete mathematics and algorithms, as well as developing specialized courses on applications of cryptography, including privacy in data analysis and decentralized computation.

Teaching Approach

I try to emphasize understanding over memorization, using concrete applications to motivate mathematical models and develop intuition alongside formal reasoning. Students come to the classroom with different backgrounds and experiences, and I believe effective teaching involves meeting them where they are and providing opportunities to master new material. I see mentorship as an important part of this process, encouraging students to ask questions, work through challenges, and develop their own approaches to solving problems.
